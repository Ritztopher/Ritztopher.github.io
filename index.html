What is the R.E.P. Tracker?


The R.E.P. (Repetition. Efficiency. Precision.) Tracker is a wholistic gym assistance device that functions as sort of a “smart” coach. The system is provided as an all-in-one case that contains each device for the system to function, and it contains a large rechargeable battery allowing for full, chord-less power support through the user’s workout.

The user can carry the system to and from the exercise environment with minimal effort. The product provides crucial information, both long and short term, for an exerciser about their workout all of which is accessible through a phone app and database!

Our R.E.P. Tracker is designed to be used by anyone, from the casual gym goer to the most dedicated bodybuilder, and it can be used in any environment, from the gym to the home.
