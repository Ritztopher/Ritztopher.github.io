What is the R.E.P. Tracker?

The R.E.P. (Repetition. Efficiency. Precision) tracker is a wholistic gym assistance device that functions as a sort of “smart” coach.
The system is provided as an all-in-one case that contains each device for the system to function, and it contains a large rechargeable battery allowing for full chord-less power support through the user’s workout.
The user can carry the system to and from the exercise environment with minimal effort.
The product provides crucial information, both long and short term, for an exerciser about their workout.
The system is designed to be used by anyone, from the casual gym goer to the most dedicated bodybuilder, and it can be used in any environment, from the gym to the home.


From heart rate monitoring to real-time audio and video feedback of exercise form technique, the R.E.P. tracker is more than a simple counting assistance.
This device is a game changer for anyone who is serious about their fitness and realising their potential.
