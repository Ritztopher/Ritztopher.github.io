Key Features:
1. Repetition Counter: The R.E.P. tracker uses advanced sensors to accurately count repetitions during exercises, ensuring users can track their progress and maintain proper form.>
2. Efficiency Analysis: Our device analyzes the user's workout efficiency by monitoring heart rate, movement patterns, and skin temperature, providing insights into how effectively they are exercising.
3. Precision Feedback: The R.E.P. tracker offers real-time audio feedback on exercise form and technique, helping users avoid injuries and maximize the benefits of their workouts.
4. User-Friendly Interface: Our system features an intuitive interface that allows users to easily access their workout data and receive personalized recommendations for improvement.
5. Camera Recordings: An easily adjustable camera allows for real-time video feedback of exercise form and technique, allowing users to visually see their form and make adjustments as needed.
6. Long Battery Life: With powerful rechargeable batteries, the R.E.P. tracker can support extended workout sessions without the need for frequent recharging.

From heart rate monitoring to real-time audio and video feedback of exercise form technique, the R.E.P. tracker is more than a simple counting assistant.
This device is a game changer for anyone who is serious about their fitness and realising their potential.
