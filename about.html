Key Features:


1. Repetition Counter:
The R.E.P. Tracker uses advanced sensors contained in the Motion Tracking barbell clamp module to accurately count repetitions during exercises, ensuring users can track their progress and maintain proper form.
2. Efficiency Analysis:
Our system analyzes the user's workout efficiency by monitoring heart rate, movement patterns, and skin temperature, providing insights into exercise effectiveness. This Performance Monitoring is accessible through a wearable armband device.
3. Precision Feedback:
Real-time User Feedback helps users improve their exercise form and technique through audio feedback during an exercise, helping to avoid injuries and maximizing the benefits of their workouts.
4. Camera Analysis:
The R.E.P. Tracker includes a built-in camera for analyzing exercise form and technique, providing visual feedback to help users improve their performance.
5. User-Friendly Interface:
The system features an intuitive interface that allows users to easily access their workout data and receive personalized recommendations for improvement.
5. Wireless Connectivity:
The R.E.P. Tracker can connect to smartphones which allows users to sync their workout data and track their progress over time via the mobile app.
6. Long Battery Life:
With powerful rechargeable batteries, the R.E.P. Tracker can support extended workout sessions without the need for frequent recharging.
7. Durable Design:
The R.E.P. Tracker is built with high-quality materials to withstand the rigors of intense workouts, ensuring it remains functional and reliable over time.


From heart rate monitoring to real-time audio and video feedback of exercise form technique, the R.E.P. Tracker is more than a simple counting assistant.

The R.E.P. Tracker is a game changer for anyone who is serious about their fitness and realizing their potential.



History:


Original Concept: The R.E.P. Tracker (originally made as the R.E.P. Counter in Foundations in ECE Design) was conceived as a solution to the common problem of inaccurate repetition counting during workouts, which can lead to inefficient training and increased risk of injury.
Our team recognized the need for a device that not only counts repetitions but also provides feedback on workout efficiency and form, leading to the development of the R.E.P. Tracker as we know it today.
